Nvidia Nemotron 3 Nano Omni multimodal open model

Nvidia released Nemotron 3 Nano Omni, a 30-billion-parameter hybrid Mamba-Transformer multimodal model with mixture-of-experts that handles text, images, video, audio, documents and charts. Nvidia claims up to 9.2x higher efficiency on video workloads versus comparable open models and ships open weights, datasets and training recipes.
