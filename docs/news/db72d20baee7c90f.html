Open Design: open-source alternative to Claude Design

A community-built open-source alternative to Anthropic's Claude Design. It auto-detects up to 15 local coding-agent CLIs (Codex, Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Kilo, etc.) and uses them as the design engine, with 31 composable skills and 72 design systems, a local daemon with filesystem access, SQLite persistence and HTML/PDF/PPTX export pipelines.
