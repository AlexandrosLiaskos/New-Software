OpenAI launches GPT-5.5 flagship model

OpenAI released GPT-5.5, a frontier model focused on agentic workflows, coding, research and document creation. It reportedly uses about a quarter of the tokens of GPT-5.4 high and a third of those of Opus 4.7, scores 82.7% on terminal-bench and 58.6% on SWE-bench Verified, and is priced at $5 per 1M input tokens and $30 per 1M output tokens.
