Anthropic Claude Design AI visual canvas in research preview

Anthropic released Claude Design, a research-preview design tool powered by Claude Opus 4.7 that generates slides, prototypes, landing pages and marketing graphics from natural-language descriptions. Outputs can be refined via chat, comments, direct edits or sliders, and exports include ZIP, PDF, standalone HTML, Canva and hand-off to Claude Code. Available to paid Claude users at launch.
