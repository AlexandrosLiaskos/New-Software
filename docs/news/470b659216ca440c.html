Hermes Agent desktop app released by Nous Research

Nous Research released an open-source native desktop app for Hermes Agent, providing a GUI to manage profiles, skills, memory, providers, cron jobs and gateways for the persistent self-evolving agent. Cross-platform installers are available for Windows, macOS and Linux.
