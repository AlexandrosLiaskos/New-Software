Codebuff switches default routing to GLM 5.1

Codebuff (and Freebuff) have switched their default model from MiniMax M2.5 to GLM 5.1. The team reports internal eval scores rising from 68% to 83% on coding benchmarks after the swap, with reported performance comparable to Claude Opus 4.6 on common tasks.

Benchmark figures (68% to 83%) are first-party Codebuff numbers reported in the video; not independently verified.
