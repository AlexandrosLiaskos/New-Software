Poolside AI releases Laguna XS.2 open-weight 33B MoE coding model

Poolside AI, a newly emerging open-source lab, released Laguna XS.2, a 33-billion-parameter mixture-of-experts model with three billion active parameters, trained on Poolside's own infrastructure. The model is designed for agentic coding and long-horizon workflows and is licensed under Apache 2.0.
