Twin: natural-language AI company and agent builder

Twin (twin.so) is a multi-agent platform that lets users describe a business or workflow in plain English and have an orchestrator, builder and runner construct and execute autonomous agents that hit APIs, drive browsers, and run on a schedule. Marketed as a no-code alternative to Zapier/n8n for end-to-end business automations such as lead generation, content repurposing and operations.

Sponsored segment, but Twin is a real product. Public launch with a $10M seed led by LocalGlobe; reports of 1M workflows automated, 100K agents deployed, 60K users. Orchestrator/builder/runner architecture with 5,000+ user-built integrations.
