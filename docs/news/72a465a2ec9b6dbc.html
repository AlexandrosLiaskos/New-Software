Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite heading to general availability on Vertex AI

Google has emailed Vertex AI customers still on Gemini Flash 2 announcing the imminent general availability of Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite, Google's most cost-effective model. Pricing is $0.25 per million input tokens and $1.50 per million output tokens, with an Arena Elo of 1432 reported at launch.

Pricing $0.25 / $1.50 per 1M input/output tokens. Available via Gemini API in Google AI Studio and Vertex AI.
